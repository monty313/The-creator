# EMERGENT SENSES LAW — PERMANENT (Law A32)

**Status:** PERMANENT COURT LAW  
**Promoted:** 2026-08-07 as **Law A32** (Monty standing order)  
**Depends on:** A6 (emergent senses concept), A28 (Opportunity Watch), A31 (goal)  
**Machine pin:** `EMERGENT_SENSES_LAW.json` · test: `tests/test_emergent_senses_law.py`  
**Case series:** CASE-0031 Sight → 0032 Feel → 0033 Taste → 0034 Hearing (`SENSES_CASE_DOCKET.md`)

The bot must develop **internal perceptual modalities** — **feel, sight, taste, hearing** — **of every official timeframe set**. Senses are **relative to edge and reasoning**, not to indicator names or absolute levels. Senses that stay probe-only (logged but never trained into the brain) **do not satisfy this law**.

---

## Scope: every official set

Official MARK sets (immutable):

- `1m,15m,30m`
- `5m,30m,1h`
- `15m,1h,4h`
- `30m,4h,1d`

Every sense evaluation must be **set-aware** (HTF support pair + LTF of that set), not a single global average of all TFs.

---

## SENSE 1 — SIGHT (structure perception) · axis **G-SIGHT**

### What the bot must see

- HTF force side on **both** support TFs per official set (**not** an average of all TFs)
- LTF velocity phase relative to that force: **with / against / flat**
- Inertia **intact vs broken** (slow period still with tide during micro dip)
- Tunnel membership: full-body **outside both rails** vs **inside** (macro vs micro)
- Multi-set **consensus vs incomplete** alignment
- Topology class: `slingshot_load | release | launch | collapse | chop`
- **Continuation vs reversal** states

### Sight fails when

The bot sits **flat on a clear bread-and-butter day** (pullback with both HTF supports trending) — that is a **perception failure**, not “no edge.”

### Court / measure hooks

- Watch complaints tagged `sight` / `miss_pb` / `miss_cont`
- Unit: structure predicates on official sets
- Measure: miss-rate drop on London/NY without dual regression

---

## SENSE 2 — FEEL (relational tension perception) · axis **G-FEEL**

### What the bot must feel

- `sign(inertia − inertia_baseline)` vs `sign(velocity − velocity_baseline)`: which clock moves which way
- **Inertia_with AND velocity_against AND G_fixed** → max tension (load building)
- **Inertia_with AND velocity_with AND thresholds met** → launch
- **Inertia_against OR G_flip** → failure or collapse
- Full-body mass outside wide rails while LTF is inside tight → **breath inside momentum**
- Efficiency/volatility regime: **nothing / tradable / great movement**; near-zero efficiency **masks fires**

### Feel fails when

The bot fires on a **lone oscillator scream** without force confirmation, or **freezes when tension is building**.

### Court / measure hooks

- Watch complaints tagged `feel` / `load_not_fired` / `false_launch`
- Measure: good-fire count up, thrash down, no breach

---

## SENSE 3 — TASTE (edge quality perception) · axis **G-TASTE**

### What the bot must taste

- Whether the current setup has **real edge** or is **noise dressed as a setup**
- Composition validity: at least one **force role AND one velocity role**; **no lone indicator**
- Cross-family agreement raises conviction; **set conflict lowers it**
- **Bread-and-butter vs marginal**
- Whether efficiency/ADX allows the velocity signal to be trusted
- **Goal distance and risk remaining:** does the day still allow fire? Is the target reachable? Is the floor safe?

### Taste fails when

Every bar is treated as **equally tradable**, or **marginal setups are fired on high-target days** when patience would pay more.

### Court / measure hooks

- Complaints tagged `taste` / `marginal` / `goal_pressure`
- Measure: hits / low_hr climb without breach; high-target band not collapse

---

## SENSE 4 — HEARING (regime and temporal perception) · axis **G-HEAR**

### What the bot must hear

- Regime shifts: **trend → chop → vol shock → undefined** (playbook must swap)
- Dual clock: fast vs slow **divergence (tension)** vs **co-alignment (launch)**
- Forward-shift displacement: mass tunnel ahead of price; **full-body clears both rails, not wick**
- Multi-set force consensus: `agree_long / agree_short / conflict / incomplete`
- **Day story:** is the reasoning still true hours later, or has structure broken?
- Wait subtype: `loaded-not-yet | no-trade | kill` — **wait is a skill with a reason**, not silence

### Hearing fails when

**Thrash-reverse** after a stop without a tide change, or **holds a stale story** into a regime shift.

### Court / measure hooks

- Complaints tagged `hearing` / `regime_shift` / `stale_story` / `london_ny`
- Measure: a13 day-share in active sessions; fewer thrash reverses
