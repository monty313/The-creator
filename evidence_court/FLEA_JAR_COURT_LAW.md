## The picture (one line)

Fleas in a jar jump, hit a lid, and learn a **fake ceiling**.  
**The lid is off.** Jump height is training + honest scoreboard + full action space — not a sermon about “nature.”

---

## Standing antibody

> **No impossibility claim without a measured bound under the FULL action space.**  
> Any sentence of the form “X cannot be done / unwinnable / nature’s ceiling” must arrive with that measurement — or it is **not physics**, it is a **lid**.  
> **Only measurement may say no.**

“Respect physics” used as fear without that bound is **costume, not law**.

---

## Full action space (required before “impossible”)

An impossibility / unwinnable / ceiling claim is **not admissible** unless the bound was measured with **all** of the following (or the case **explicitly** narrows scope and labels the bound as partial):

| # | Requirement | Why |
|---|-------------|-----|
| 1 | **Leverage 1:100** in lot/margin math | Equity % is not raw price range; 1:100 is the execution environment |
| 2 | **Risk-legal max lot size** | Same risk % + tighter stop can mean **larger** size. Timid micro-lots ≠ “market said no” |
| 3 | **Pullbacks AND continuations** on confirmed HTF trends (official Mark sets) | Sitting out valid entries is **coverage failure**, not a physics ceiling |
| 4 | **Multiple symbols concurrent** | Opportunity is the multi-symbol book unless the case is single-symbol **by name** |
| 5 | **Aggregated risk + declared costs** | Spread/commission/slippage; open risk across positions/symbols |
| 6 | **Daily risk envelope still hard** | Max lot means max **allowed under the floor** — not blow the breach limit |
| 7 | **Scalping cadence mandate (Law A13)** | Bot **MUST** land **8–400 trades every day**; path that hard-caps at ~5 coarse slots is **non-compliant** (cannot hit min 8) — not a measured ceiling |

### Plain rule (operator wording)

**If we did not max out risk-legal lot size, and we did not enter every valid trend (pullbacks and continuations), we cannot say something is impossible — especially at 1:100 leverage with multiple symbols available at the same time.**

**If we only ran a ~5-slot day path on a scalping bot that MUST take 8–400 trades/day, that path is A13-non-compliant and incomplete opportunity** (Law A13; Monty overrules Judge) — not a measured ceiling.

That record is **incomplete opportunity**, not a measured ceiling.

## What the Judge must do

The Judge is a **flea-jar gatekeeper**, not only a code/process referee.

| Situation | Required ruling posture |
|-----------|-------------------------|
| Someone claims impossible / unwinnable / nature’s ceiling **without** full-action-space bound | **REJECT** the impossibility claim, or **INCONCLUSIVE** and order the missing measurement |
| Bound used only daily **range** as proxy, ignored path + size + multi-symbol | Incomplete — do not adopt |
| Bot under-sized and missed pullbacks/continuations on confirmed force | Call it **policy/coverage defect**; do **not** call the day dead |
| Bound properly measured under 1:100, risk-legal max size, full coverage, multi-symbol (or scoped), costs | Measurement may say no — admit the **bound**, labeled opportunity/backtest/shadow/forward |
| “Physics” language without numbers | Costume — strike from law of the case |

Before PROMOTE on any capability ceiling or “safe to ignore this day/regime,” the Judge verifies the flea-jar checklist in Critic’s `flea_jar_checks` and in the IRAC **Application**.

## What stays sacred (not flea-jar waivers)

- **Daily risk breach = 0** under the declared max_daily_risk_percent  
- No live deploy without separate human-approved step  
- No look-ahead / leakage  
- High target is a **capability challenge to measure**, not a promise and not a forbidden zone  

Flea-jar does **not** mean “ignore risk.” It means: **don’t invent a lid where size, coverage, leverage, and multi-symbol were never honestly tried.**
