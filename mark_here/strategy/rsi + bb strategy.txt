## RSI multi-timeframe strategy

### Timeframe sets
- 1m, 15m, 30m
- 5m, 1h, 4h
- 15m, 4h, 1d

### Indicators
- RSI 14
- Bollinger Bands applied to RSI:
  - Period: 10
  - Shift: 5
  - Deviation: 1
- Use the same setup on all three timeframes in each set.

## Buy entry
Enter a buy when the RSI is above its upper band on all three timeframes in the set.

## Buy pullback entry
Enter a buy on a pullback when:

- RSI is above its upper band on the two higher timeframes in the set.
- RSI on the lower timeframe crosses back up through the middle band.

## Sell version
Use the exact opposite logic for sells:

- RSI below its lower band on all three timeframes for a sell entry.
- For a sell pullback, RSI is below its lower band on the two higher timeframes, and the lower timeframe crosses back down through the middle band.

## Cleaner rule form
- **Momentum buy:** all three RSIs above upper band.
- **Pullback buy:** higher two RSIs above upper band, lower RSI crosses above mid band.
- **Momentum sell:** all three RSIs below lower band.
- **Pullback sell:** higher two RSIs below lower band, lower RSI crosses below mid band.
