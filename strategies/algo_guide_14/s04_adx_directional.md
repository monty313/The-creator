 The Concept 
 The Average Directional Index (ADX) measures trend STRENGTH, not direction. A high ADX means a strong trend exists. A low ADX means the market is ranging — neither bulls nor bears are in control. The +DI (Positive Directional Indicator) and −DI (Negative Directional Indicator) tell you the DIRECTION. The ADX tells you whether it is worth trading that direction. Used correctly, ADX is the most powerful filter in trend trading: it tells you when NOT to trade. 

 How to Use It 

 Entry Rules 

 Long condition: +DI crosses ABOVE −DI, AND ADX is above 25 (or rising toward 25 from below with momentum). 

 Short condition: −DI crosses ABOVE +DI, AND ADX is above 25. 

 Strongest setups: ADX is rising (not just above 25, but still climbing). This means the trend is accelerating. 

 ADX between 25–40: healthy trend. ADX above 40: very strong trend — be careful about entering late. 

 ADX below 25: stay out of directional trades entirely. Look for range-bound strategies instead. 

 Stop Loss and Exit 

 Stop loss: below the most recent swing low (long) or above the most recent swing high (short). 

 Exit signal: −DI crosses back above +DI (for a long trade). This is the directional reversal signal. 

 Also exit if ADX begins to decline sharply from above 40 — the trend is exhausting itself. 

 Trailing option: trail stop to the previous bar's low (for longs), advancing each bar the trade stays open. 

 Worked Example 
 Gold is in a choppy market with ADX at 18 — no trade. Three weeks later, ADX has climbed to 28 and +DI (32) has crossed above −DI (18). Long entry on the next open. Stop at the previous swing low. Over the next 5 weeks gold trends up steadily. ADX reaches 42 then starts declining. −DI rises back above +DI — exit signal. Trade closed for a 4.8% gain. 

 Common Mistakes 

 Trading when ADX is below 25 — this is the most common ADX mistake. Below 25 means no trend. MA crossovers and breakouts in this environment fail constantly. 

 Confusing ADX direction with trade direction — ADX falling does NOT mean go short. ADX measures strength, not direction. −DI above +DI signals short direction. 

 Entering when ADX is already above 50 — by this point the trend is often in its final stage. The best entries are when ADX is 20–35 and rising.
